01 / About Eventure

Better events start with a connected experience.

Eventure began with a simple belief: finding an event and running one should not feel like separate worlds.

We bring the attendee journey and organizer workflow together so both sides can move with more clarity—from discovery and ticketing to payment review, attendee management, and reporting.

03 / Platform reliability

Checkout stays coordinated—even when plans change.

A checkout touches more than a ticket. Seats, vouchers, coupons, and points need to stay in agreement when an order is placed, expires, is cancelled, or is rejected. Eventure handles those changes together in one serializable database transaction.

Payments follow a deliberate human-review flow: attendees submit proof, then organizers approve or reject it.
